Changes in investment practices and valuation frameworks

Assuming higher cost of capital

  • Elevated discount rates for tighter conditions
  • Higher hurdle rates for capital allocation

More conservative valuation approach

  • More weight on downside and bear-case scenarios
  • Less reliance on long-dated terminal values

Value over growth momentum

  • Pivoting toward earnings-supported valuations
  • Favoring unit economics over scale narratives

Cash flow & balance sheet focus

  • Free cash flow as a key screening metric
  • Reviewing leverage and refinancing schedules

Bottom-up stock selection

  • Less macro-driven top-down factor exposure
  • Deeper fundamental research on each security

Shorter-term orientation

  • Narrower forecasting windows for margins
  • More frequent reassessment of position sizing
