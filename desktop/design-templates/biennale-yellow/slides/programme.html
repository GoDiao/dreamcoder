Strands · 2026
Programme
Six interlocking strands run across the year. Each is independently curated, but every strand answers to the same question: what does light know that we don't?
Strand · Title · Anchor
01

Slow Atmospheres

A reading room of long-form essays, drawings and weather notebooks about late-afternoon yellow.

02

Public Form

Three commissions in three pavilions, each studying how a public square wears its light.

03

Field Notes

A residency that brings artists, architects and meteorologists together for a hundred days.

04

Quiet Editions

A publishing strand that prints only what asks to be read in daylight, on warm paper.

05

Open Conversations

Twelve evenings of public talks, paired with a meal and one question about weather.
