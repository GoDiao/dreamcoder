# Sponsorship

DreamCoder is maintained by one person in their spare time. Companies and individuals are welcome to sponsor its development. API providers, model platforms and developer tools are all welcome to get in touch.

## Tiers

| Tier | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| **Sponsor** | Your logo, a short description and a link in the README's Sponsors section and on the website. |
| **Platinum sponsor** | Everything above, plus a banner at the top of the README and a provider preset in the DreamCoder app, labelled "Sponsor". |

Pricing depends on placement and duration, and is agreed case by case. The minimum term is 3 months.

## Rules

- **Sponsorship doesn't buy product decisions.** Sponsors can't ask for changes to features, defaults or the roadmap. A provider preset only fills in the setup for users faster; it never becomes the default provider.
- **We don't accept** gambling, adult content, or sites buying links for search ranking.
- **The maintainer reviews what is shown.** Sponsors provide the logo, description and link, and the maintainer may ask for changes.
- **When a sponsorship ends or breaks these rules,** we remove it from the README and website and retire the provider preset. Provider configurations users have already saved keep working.
- **The maintainer may decline or end any sponsorship.**
